Ilakir was made in order to assist customers to access our services easily without confusion with Aires Solutions in India. Location and Community Ilakir is located in Eldoret, Kenya along the Eldoret-Iten road. Culture and Tradition We try our best to be as professional when handlling our clients to ensure a positive experience for all parties involved. Values We value integrity and efficiency in everything we do. We are also commited to improving the society as evidenced from some of our former projects such as nakili and wajibika. Resources and Techniques We create our software products locally using both human programmers and AI but source our hardware products from China. Any products we design we send to be manufactured in China so that we can provide the best value for our clients at a cost they are comfortable with.
